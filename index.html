The Challenge

A trust can last 50 years. Your voice can too.

Every year, trustees face decisions the trust document never anticipated. A grandchild wants to start a company. A beneficiary faces addiction. The world changes in ways no one could have foreseen.

In those moments, trustees must decide alone — guided only by static legal language written years or decades earlier. The one person who truly knew the answer is no longer here.

The Process

Three steps to a lasting voice.

01
The Interview

In a single structured day, our specialists guide clients through a comprehensive curriculum — values, family dynamics, financial philosophy, specific scenarios, and forward-looking questions designed to anticipate decisions that may arise decades into the future. Developed with input from trust attorneys, private wealth advisors, and corporate trustees — so the questions we ask reflect what actually matters when it counts. Every session is professionally recorded on video.

02
Your Personal Voice Agent

The recorded interviews are used to build a private Voice Agent — a secure, searchable system built exclusively from the grantor's own words. Powered by AI, it understands how this specific person thought and what they prioritized. It retrieves and surfaces exactly what the grantor said on relevant topics — grounded entirely in their recorded words, never a generated guess. This is the technology that makes the grantor's own words available on demand, for as long as the trust endures.

03
The Voice, Forever

When a trustee or beneficiary asks a question, the Voice Agent responds with a written answer drawn from the grantor's own recorded words — and the grantor's video appears, paused at the exact moment they spoke to that topic. One tap and they see and hear it for themselves. The answer is not an interpretation. It is the grantor themselves, ready to speak.

Why It Matters

The case for preserving your voice

Estate planning documents explain what should happen. The Trust Voice preserves why.

  • — Litigation deterrence. A contemporaneous, well-reasoned record of your own thinking makes a future contest a harder, costlier case to bring. It won't function as legal evidence itself, but it removes the ambiguity contests are built on — and can save tens of thousands of dollars in legal fees, mediation, or litigation before a dispute ever starts.
  • — Emotional continuity. Beneficiaries don't just read your wishes. They see and hear you delivering them.
  • — Real support for your trustee's hardest decisions. The person who administers your trust will face difficult, gray-area requests and quiet pressure to say yes. Your recorded context gives them something real to lean on in those moments — clarity when they need it most, and the confidence that they are honoring your intentions.
  • — A living Letter of Wishes. Queryable and verifiable, it stays relevant across decades.

Completely Private & Siloed

Each client's Voice Agent is entirely isolated. No data from one client's trust is ever accessible to another. The system is built on enterprise-grade infrastructure with bank-level security protocols.

Security Standard Each agent operates in a dedicated, siloed environment. Access is restricted to authorized trustees and named parties only — protected by multi-factor authentication and full audit logging.
Designed for the Long Term

The Trust Voice is built with perpetuity in mind. Your client's voice and intentions are preserved in a private virtual vault — a secure, redundant archive — available for as long as the trust remains active, whether that is 10 years or 60.

Long-Term Continuity All video archives are stored redundantly across multiple data centers within AWS’s U.S. infrastructure, with documented succession plans ensuring continuity of access regardless of changes in technology or business conditions.
