The Challenge

A trust can last 50 years. Your voice can too.

Every year, trustees face decisions the trust document never anticipated. A grandchild wants to start a company. A beneficiary faces addiction. The world changes in ways no attorney could have foreseen.

In those moments, trustees must decide alone — guided only by static legal language written years or decades earlier. The one person who truly knew the answer is no longer here.

The Process

Three steps to a lasting voice.

01
The Interview

In a single structured day, our specialists guide clients through a comprehensive curriculum — values, family dynamics, financial philosophy, specific scenarios, and forward-looking questions designed to anticipate decisions that may arise decades into the future. Every session is professionally recorded on video.

02
The AI Agent

The recorded interviews are used to build a private AI agent — built exclusively from the client's own words, values, and intentions. The agent understands how this specific person thought, what they prioritized, and how they would approach decisions they never anticipated.

03
The Voice, Forever

When a trustee or beneficiary asks a question, the agent provides a written answer drawn directly from the client's recorded responses — with a citation link to the exact video segment. Anyone can see, in the grantor's own words and face, that this was truly their position.

What Makes Us Different

Not an opinion.
Evidence.

Every answer the agent provides is anchored to video of the grantor — verified, timestamped, and unchallengeable.

Video Citation on Every Answer

When the agent responds to a question, it links directly to the segment of the recorded interview from which the answer was drawn. Trustees, beneficiaries, and attorneys can watch the grantor speak those words themselves.

Completely Private & Siloed

Each client's agent is entirely isolated. No data from one client's trust is ever accessible to another. The system is built on enterprise-grade infrastructure with bank-level security protocols.

Security Standard Each agent operates in a dedicated, siloed environment. Access is restricted to authorized trustees and named parties only — protected by multi-factor authentication and full audit logging.
Designed for the Long Term

The Trust Voice is built with perpetuity in mind. Your client's voice and intentions are preserved in a secure, redundant archive — available for as long as the trust remains active, whether that is 10 years or 60.

Long-Term Continuity All video archives are maintained in geographically redundant data centers with documented succession plans ensuring continuity of access regardless of changes in technology or business conditions.
