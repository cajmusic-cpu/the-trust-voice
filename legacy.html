The process

Three steps to a lasting voice.

01

The interview

In a single structured day, our specialists lead you through a comprehensive curriculum — values, family dynamics, financial philosophy, and forward-looking scenarios. Every session is professionally recorded on video.

02

Your private archive

Your recordings become a secure, searchable archive built exclusively from your own words — isolated to your family, protected with bank-level security, and maintained for as long as the trust endures.

03

Your voice, on demand

When a trustee or beneficiary asks a question, the answer arrives in your recorded words — and your video appears, paused at the exact moment you spoke to that topic. One tap, and they see and hear it for themselves.

Why it matters

The case for preserving your voice

Estate planning documents explain what should happen. The Trust Voice preserves why.

Emotional continuity

Beneficiaries don’t just read your wishes. They see and hear you delivering them — a generation from now, and the one after that.

Real support for hard decisions

Whoever administers your trust will face gray-area requests and quiet pressure to say yes. Your recorded context gives them something real to lean on — and the confidence that they are honoring your intentions.

Litigation deterrence

A contemporaneous, well-reasoned record of your own thinking makes a future contest a harder, costlier case to bring. It removes the ambiguity that disputes are built on — before one ever starts.
